Arrival → flowers → shared smiles → dinner → table game and laughter → moving group → final photograph. The actual footage determined the structure: clips 14–16 show a wooden tower game rather than dinner. Text fades in gently. The ending uses a dark warm matte to preserve the group's full width and provide a separate, readable area for the greeting without covering faces.

No people, faces or backgrounds were synthesized or replaced. Clips remain at real-time speed; approximately 30fps source material was not forced into slow motion. Video fills the vertical frame without additional subject cropping. Flower still is cropped for 9:16. The final group photo removes empty ceiling and keeps the full horizontal field; the minimal push-in stays inside the surrounding matte.
